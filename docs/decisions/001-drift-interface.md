# Drift is the reflection interface

Status: accepted — 4 August 2026

The three-way interface prototype tested Drift, Landing, and Focus against the product
handover. Drift was selected decisively.

Drift keeps the spatial field as the interface, gives typography and motion priority,
and limits permanent chrome to the wordmark. This best supports the intended calm,
canvas-first reflection experience without making loose thoughts feel like an inbox or
placing a reading mode between the user and the space.

Amended 4 August 2026: Quick Capture was removed. Enter now opens spatial creation at
the pointer, falling back to the viewport center. This keeps all thought creation in the
spatial field.

The complete comparison remains available on the `prototype/space-variants` branch at
commit `0c4e2fe`.
