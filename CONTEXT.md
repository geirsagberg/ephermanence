# Ephermanence

Ephermanence is a spatial canvas for capturing thoughts and discovering relationships
through proximity and movement.

## Language

**Spatial field**:
The open canvas in which thoughts are placed, moved, and encountered.

**Thought**:
A short piece of captured writing with a position and visual presence in the spatial
field.

**Bond**:
A visible relationship between two thoughts formed by bringing them into contact.

**Drift**:
The reflection experience in which the spatial field itself remains the primary
interface, with minimal permanent chrome.
